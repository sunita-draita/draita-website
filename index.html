AI Governance • Responsible Automation • Digital Transformation

Navigate AI and automation with clarity, governance, and confidence.

Draita helps organizations improve operations and adopt emerging technology responsibly by combining process clarity, governance discipline, and practical delivery experience. We help business and technology teams understand what is being used, where governance is missing, and what needs to be improved before risk, complexity, or audit pressure forces the conversation.

01

AI Governance & Readiness

“Are we adopting AI responsibly, and where are our governance gaps?”

  • AI Landscape & Risk Assessment: Identify known and emerging AI use cases, ownership gaps, and areas of governance risk.
  • Governance Framework & Controls: Define practical policies, roles, decision rights, controls, and oversight aligned to business needs.
  • Evidence & Regulatory Readiness: Assess documentation, controls, and evidence against relevant governance and regulatory expectations.
02

Process & Automation Advisory

“Where should we improve, automate, or use AI?”

  • Process Discovery & Optimization: Map workflows, bottlenecks, handoffs, exceptions, and operational pain points.
  • Automation & AI Opportunity Assessment: Evaluate where workflow automation, RPA, AI, or human-led redesign provides the best fit.
  • Risk, Control & Efficiency Design: Improve speed and scalability without introducing unnecessary operational or governance risk.
03

Transformation & Governance-Led Delivery

“How do we actually get this transformation delivered?”

  • Transformation Delivery & PMO: Establish delivery structure, governance, milestones, dependencies, risks, and decision-making.
  • Technology & Vendor Evaluation: Assess solution options, vendors, implementation approaches, and enterprise fit.
  • Stakeholder & Change Alignment: Bring business, technology, operations, governance, and leadership around a common delivery path.
